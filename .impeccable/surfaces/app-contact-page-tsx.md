# Contact the Editorial Office

Mode: Operate

## Purpose
Route manuscript pitches, errata, metadata harvesting, verification and security disclosure to the right channel.

## First useful result
A visitor identifies the right contact for their request and reaches it in one action.

## Direction
Plain labeled list of contact purposes with direct links; the only named person is the Editor-in-Chief.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Link targets, security.txt reference, mobile layout, and keyboard focus.
