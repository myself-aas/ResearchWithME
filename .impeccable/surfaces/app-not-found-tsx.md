# Not Found

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Help a visitor recover from a missing address.

## First useful result
A visitor understands the page is missing and reaches the platform or paper archive in one action.

## Direction
A calm editorial dead end with a strong title, plain recovery actions, and no decorative error illustration.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Keyboard focus, readable contrast, narrow viewport, and assistive-technology announcement.
