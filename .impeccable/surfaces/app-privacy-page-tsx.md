# Privacy Policy

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Explain what reader and contributor data the platform handles and why.

## First useful result
A reader learns in plain language what is stored, where, and how to remove it.

## Direction
Long-form serif policy with clear headings; statements match what the code actually does.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Heading order, accuracy against current data handling, long lines at 320px, and keyboard focus.
