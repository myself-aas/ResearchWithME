# Paper Archive

Mode: Operate

## Purpose
Find an open-access paper by field, DOI, author, keyword, or date.

## First useful result
A scholar narrows the archive to a relevant paper and opens a dedicated reading surface.

## Direction
Standard filter and sort controls lead. The editorial palette supplies the shell, type, density, and DOI/citation signature; the archive never becomes a terminal costume.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Search, topic and sort filters, empty results, local images, bookmark persistence, narrow viewport, and keyboard focus.
