# Contributor Profile

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Showcase a researcher’s structured contributions, published editorial articles, and non-vanity scholarly recognition metrics.

## First useful result
A visitor evaluates a contributor’s substantive body of work and opens their published reviews or evidence contributions.

## Direction
Highlight helpful reviews, references, saves, evidence reuse, and replications instead of social vanity metrics.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow cleanly to 360px without horizontal overflow.

## Checks
Contributor metrics grid on mobile, contributions list, editorial articles bridge, and 404 on unknown handles.
