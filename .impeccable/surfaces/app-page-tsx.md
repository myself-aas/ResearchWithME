# Home / Article Feed

Mode: Persuade

## Purpose
Help researchers find a rigorous article quickly and understand why the publication is trustworthy.

## First useful result
A reader lands on a readable lead article, can search or filter the catalog, and opens a dedicated article page without a modal interrupting the task.

## Direction
Use the archival editorial system: warm parchment ground, Newsreader display type, amber actions, emerald verification cues, and a calm reading measure. Keep the article hierarchy bold and the feed compact but legible.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Search, category and format filters, bookmark persistence, featured link, author link, mobile navigation, narrow viewport, and reduced motion.
