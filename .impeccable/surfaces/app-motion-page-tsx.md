# Motion Lab

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Reference the motion system: principles, tokens, loading contracts and lazy-loading primitives.

## First useful result
A contributor finds a token or primitive and sees it demonstrated live.

## Direction
Design-system reference page, noindex, linked from the footer. Demos honour full, reduced and static motion modes.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Full, reduced and static modes, token tables at 320px, keyboard focus, and noindex robots metadata.
