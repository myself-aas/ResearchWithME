# PDF Editions

Mode: Operate

## Purpose
Find and download a curated, print-ready compendium.

## First useful result
A librarian or researcher identifies an edition and receives a clear download or failure state.

## Direction
Use an archival shelf rhythm with one card per edition. Metadata is separated by rules and spacing rather than nested boxes.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Download progress, unavailable files, long editor names, mobile metadata, and keyboard focus.
