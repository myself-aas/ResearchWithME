# Paper Reader

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Understand a structured paper from abstract through methods, findings, references, and reproducibility metadata.

## First useful result
A reader can scan the section hierarchy, inspect citation data, and copy or download the paper without losing place.

## Direction
The world owns the masthead, metadata rail, section openers, and code/table setting. The reading column stays calm at 65–75ch with real contrast.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Long titles, missing figures, DOI copy, PDF download failure, mobile tables, related links, and reduced motion.
