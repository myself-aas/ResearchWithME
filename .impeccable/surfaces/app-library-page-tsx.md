# Personal Research Library and Goals

Mode: Operate

## Purpose
Manage saved sources, articles, and contributions across collections while tracking calm Personal Research OS goals.

## First useful result
A researcher filters saved items by collection, removes stale entries, and updates or creates a structured research goal.

## Direction
Pair a clean collection view with a non-manipulative progress tracker focused on substantive research milestones rather than vanity streaks.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow cleanly to 360px without horizontal overflow.
- Minimum 44px tap targets on interactive controls.

## Checks
Empty library state, collection filtering, item removal, goal creation, and progress increment to completion.
