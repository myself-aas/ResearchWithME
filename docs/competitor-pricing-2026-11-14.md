Checked 2026-11-14. Re-verify before using any figure in public copy.

How to read this: every competitor price below was seen in web search results on the date above. Most come from
third-party comparison articles and marketplace listing pages, not from the vendor's own pricing page; the source
column says which. A price not listed here was not found, which is not the same as the product being free or
absent. Marketplace prices are asking prices; nothing here shows how many copies anyone sells.
