Subject: Pre-approval question: two automated manuscript-checking tools (SaaS)

Hello,

I run the store for ResearchWithMe (https://researchwith.me), a free archive of research reviews. The store has no products yet; the first will be a PDF download. Your
prohibited-products page asks sellers to check before selling when unsure, so I would like to confirm two software
tools before I build them. Both are fully automated web tools: the buyer uploads their own document and receives
a report within minutes. No person reads, edits, or comments on anything, and neither tool writes or rewrites any
text.

1. Manuscript Check (planned price about $8 per report)
   The buyer uploads their own research manuscript. Software checks it against fixed rules and returns a report:
   missing or out-of-order sections, items of public reporting guidelines (CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE) that are not
   reported, citations that do not match the reference list, statistics that are internally inconsistent, numbers
   in the abstract that differ from the body, claims with no supporting result, and paragraphs with no clear
   point. Each finding points to the place in the text. The report states that it is produced by software and is
   not peer review. The tool gives no suggested rewrites and produces no new text.

2. Response-to-Reviewers Check (planned price about $8 per report)
   An author who is revising a paper uploads the journal reviewers' comments and the response letter the author
   has already written. Software lists every reviewer comment and marks each as answered, partly answered, or not
   answered, quoting the matching passage of the author's letter. It does not draft, complete, or reword the
   response.

My questions:

a) Are these acceptable as "Software & SaaS", and not treated as "Services of any kind"?
b) Does either fall under "Homework/Essay mills"? They only check a document the buyer wrote and never generate
   or alter text.
c) Is there any wording you would want on, or kept off, the product pages?

Both would be sold as one-time purchases of a single automated run, fulfilled on my site immediately after
payment. I am happy to send mock-ups or a sample report.
