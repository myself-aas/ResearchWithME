# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users: Research scientists, systems software engineers, clinical practitioners, technology analysts, and inquisitive readers seeking rigorous, reproducible peer breakdowns, codebase teardowns, and analytical essays without academic paywalls or marketing hype.

## Product Purpose

ResearchWithMe publishes deep technical teardowns of open-source GitHub repositories and analytical audits of peer-reviewed literature across STEM, health sciences, sociology, and quantitative economics. It bridges high-rigor scholarly discourse with engineering practice.

## Positioning

Unlike superficial tech newsletters or gated academic journals charging commercial APCs, ResearchWithMe combines line-by-line systems engineering teardowns (such as vLLM KV-cache profiling and DeepSeek-R1 emergent reasoning) with verified academic researcher dossiers (ORCID, GitHub, and Institutional .edu validation) under Diamond Open Access (CC BY 4.0).

## Operating Context

- Readers review articles on desktop and mobile browsers, seeking key takeaways, reproducible data repositories, and DOI links.
- Verified domain contributors draft reviews, teardowns, and critiques in the Writer & Reviewer Studio.
- Researchers export citations across APA, Chicago, MLA, BibTeX, and RIS formats.
- Scholars query literature via the AI Literature Synthesizer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dedicated full-page portals with unique URLs: `/write`, `/verify`, `/bookmarks`, `/cite`, `/assistant`, `/submit`, `/authors`, `/editions`.
- Real OAuth and Domain Verification flow (ORCID, GitHub, Institutional Email, Gmail reader).
- Interactive reading features: reading list bookmarking, comment discourse, citation copying, social sharing (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, and native mobile share sheets).
- Zero pop-up discipline: all workflows live on dedicated pages or non-intrusive floating toasts.
- Fast Next.js 15 App Router architecture with Tailwind CSS.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: ResearchWithMe
- Aesthetic archetype: Serious scholarly publication meets high-performance engineering documentation.
- Palette: Warm parchment and deep stone neutrals, rich scholarly amber, emerald open-access accents, obsidian text.
- Typography: Editorial serif headings with clean monospace accents for data/code, sans body.

## Evidence on Hand

- Peer-reviewed research publications catalog with DOI resolution and data archive links.
- 6 active domain columns: Research Reviews, GitHub Repo Teardowns, STEM & Tech, Health & Medicine, Sociology & Culture, Data & Policy.
- Verified author dossiers (Dr. Elena Rostova, Maya Lin, Dr. Sarah Chen, Alex Taylor).
- Real citation generator supporting 6 academic citation standards.

## Product Principles

1. Rigor over speed: Every article must articulate verified empirical findings and reproducible claims.
2. Uncompromised open access: Zero paywalls, zero behavioral tracking, CC BY 4.0 license.
3. Domain expertise verified at source: Contributors validate identities via ORCID, GitHub maintainer credentials, or institutional faculty affiliation.
4. Editorial craft: Interface elements exist to serve comprehension, reading stamina, and academic discourse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA / AAA contrast standards across dark and light surfaces.
- Accessible focus rings, semantic HTML landmarks (`header`, `main`, `article`, `section`, `footer`), keyboard navigation.
- Responsive typography with comfortable line heights (1.75) and reading measure (65–75ch).
