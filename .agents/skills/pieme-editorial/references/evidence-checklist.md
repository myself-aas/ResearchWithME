# Evidence review for πeme

Use the human CITABLE checklist in `docs/ResearchWithMe_Template.md`. For each consequential claim, ask:

- Can a reader locate the primary paper, commit, dataset or experiment? Verify the link and specific passage; a plausible-looking DOI is not verification.
- Is this the author's assertion, a third-party citation, or an experiment the reviewer ran? Keep those categories distinct.
- Does the cited version support the exact number, mechanism and scope? Record negative results and uncertainty.
- Are byline, affiliations, funding and dates supported by an authorized source? Omit when unknown.
- Does the title/summary describe the visible article instead of promising rich results or AI citations?
- Are references, links, images, diagrams and any structured data present and accurate in the **published** representation? Check rendered HTML and Markdown after deployment.

Automated local checks can find heading or link syntax, not verify scientific truth. Stop publication on invented evidence; report unresolved source and consent questions to the editor.
