# Editorial Board

Mode: Read

## Purpose
Understand who safeguards the platform and how review standards work.

## First useful result
A reader identifies an editor, their domain, and the verification principles without decorative clutter.

## Direction
Editor names lead. Icons sit inline with headings; the three principles read as an editorial numbered argument, not equal icon cards.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Reflow to 320px without horizontal scrolling.
- No kickers above headings, gradient text, glass as decoration, nested cards, or intrusive popups.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states explain what happened and what to do next.
- Local assets and honest demo labels; no fabricated live verification claims.

## Checks
Long institution names, ORCID overflow, mobile layout, and contrast on dark surfaces.
